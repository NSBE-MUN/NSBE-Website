About Us

“Excellence through Unity and Innovation.”

Mission: To increase the number of culturally responsible Black engineers who excel academically, succeed professionally, and positively impact the community.

We are a community of students who believe that everyone deserves to feel supported, represented, and empowered throughout their academic and professional journey.

While rooted in engineering, NSBE MUN is open to all Black students in STEM at Memorial University, as well as anyone who wants to support our mission. We create opportunities for students to connect, grow, and thrive through community-building, professional development, mentorship, social events, and meaningful connections.

We believe diversity is more than representation. It is about building a community where people feel valued, included, and empowered to pursue their goals.

Come as you are. Find your people. Build your future.
